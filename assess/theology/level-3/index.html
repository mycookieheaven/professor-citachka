Cumulative level test: Reading Catholic Sources: Revelation, Worship, and Public Responsibility

Check understanding, separately from reading

12 questions · 80% to pass · no timer. This longer test revisits both units in the level. These authored questions assess recognition and application of the taught distinctions, not professional qualification. You may review the lessons and retry. Repeat attempts reuse the question bank; a remembered answer is not proof of transfer to a new situation.

Saving a lesson records reading only. Previous lesson completions have not been converted into passing scores. You can continue reading without a pass; the assessment remains unpassed.

Question 1

A student extracts a rhetorical question from Romans 6, calls it an infallible command, and uses it to defend wrongdoing. What two checks expose the mistake?

Question 2

A guide says the pope invented the Eucharistic tradition because a modern council explains it. Which source comparison corrects both the chronology of evidence and the model of authority?

Question 3

A speaker uses Paul’s wilderness comparison to claim that Christians are morally immune and Jews are collectively rejected. Which combined response follows the assigned sources?

Question 4

A historian asks whether all Catholic institutions have always respected religious freedom. A student supplies only the council’s claim of continuity. What evidence gap remains?

Question 5

A parish says lawful celebration excuses ignoring humiliation of poor members and barriers to participation. Which response preserves the relevant distinctions?

Question 6

A minority congregation holds beliefs a Catholic official rejects. The official therefore proposes forcing its members to attend Catholic worship. Which argument blocks the inference without requiring relativism?

Question 7

A teacher says every new doctrinal term proves betrayal, but accepts a familiar phrase whose meaning has been reversed. What method should replace both judgments?

Question 8

An observer judges a seated worshipper spiritually unworthy and claims a bishop’s administrative status makes that judgment infallible. What are the independent errors?

Question 9

A study group uses Romans 6 to say baptism buys salvation through human effort, while another member says grace makes action irrelevant. Which reconstruction addresses both?

Question 10

A proposed shelter requires a creed for meals and segregates guests who cannot donate. Which source-based policy revision joins the level’s social and sacramental reasoning?

Question 11

A paper cites a Bible footnote as though it were a verse and quotes a Catholic declaration as a complete account of Jewish self-understanding. What revision improves both uses of evidence?

Question 12

A cumulative essay must explain a disputed public-order restriction while remaining faithful to Catholic teaching. Which structure best integrates method and application?
