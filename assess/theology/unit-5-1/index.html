Question 1

A parish group debates whether to print a leaflet promising that a nine-day devotion will obtain a specific favour. One member cites the Catechism on prayer; another says that since God already knows our needs, petition is unnecessary. Which assessment addresses both positions using this level's sources?

Question 2

A speaker quotes “money is the root of all evil” to argue that Christians may not hold savings accounts. A listener checks the verse and finds a different sentence. Identify the failure mode and the correct way to use the passage.

Question 3

A parish council proposes to chant an unapproved local translation of the eucharistic prayer, arguing that the approved text is hard for older parishioners to follow, and that the liturgy is the community's own prayer. Which reasoning applies?

Question 4

A participant in an ecumenical study day says that since the sixteenth century the Catholic Church has invented the doctrine of the real presence to justify a medieval practice, and that the earliest Christians regarded the bread and wine as symbols only. Which sources bear on the claim?

Question 5

A Catholic legislator supports a policy that increases the burden of a benefit scheme on low-wage workers while invoking subsidiarity as the reason, and a colleague invokes solidarity to oppose it. What does this level's material require of both?
