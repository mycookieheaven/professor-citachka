Question 1

A learner's forearm patch test for a new body lotion was negative, so she plans to use the same lotion on her eyelid area because the ingredients are identical. Using the regional anatomy from this unit, what is the correct reasoning?

Question 2

A claim states that a moisturiser cut trans-epidermal water loss by 15 per cent after two weeks on the forearm, with no other detail. A learner wants to compare it with a claim of a 25 per cent hydration increase on the cheek. What is the correct appraisal?

Question 3

A brand markets a barrier serum, citing a trial in which 30 participants used the product and their barrier measurements improved significantly over eight weeks. There was no comparison group. What is the strongest reading?

Question 4

A 32-participant split-face study reports that the antioxidant side improved more than the placebo side, but it does not state the randomisation method, the blinding of the assessor, or the number analysed per side. What is the appropriate conclusion?

Question 5

A reviewer dismisses a trial because 12 of its 40 randomised participants did not complete the study, arguing that a per-protocol analysis of the 28 completers is the scientifically clean approach. How should that argument be assessed?
