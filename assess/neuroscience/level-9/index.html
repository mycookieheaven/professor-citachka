Question 1

Hippocampal theta in a rat runs at 7 Hz while gamma runs at 60 Hz. During a spatial choice, gamma amplitude is greatest at the trough of theta. If the coupling is real, how many gamma cycles fit within one theta cycle, and what would a report that one gamma burst occurs per theta cycle imply about the coupling?

Question 2

Participants perform a contrast discrimination task. Those with high pre-stimulus tonic pupil diameter show worse discrimination and larger response variability, while those with intermediate diameter perform best, and a phasic pupil response to the stimulus predicts faster correct responses. Which framework organises these results?

Question 3

Rats receive selective immunotoxic lesions of basal forebrain cholinergic neurons and are tested on a five-choice serial reaction time task. They make more omission errors and have slower correct responses, but accuracy on trials they complete is unchanged, and performance falls further when the inter-trial interval is made variable. What is the best interpretation?

Question 4

A drug that is a substrate of P-glycoprotein at the blood-brain barrier is given with a second drug that inhibits P-glycoprotein. Brain concentrations of the first drug rise tenfold while plasma concentrations rise only 1.3-fold. A commentary describes this as proof that the barrier is failing in patients with inflammation. What is wrong with the commentary?

Question 5

Patients receiving interferon-alpha for hepatitis C develop depression at a higher rate than controls, and pretreatment with an antidepressant reduces the incidence. A review concludes that inflammation causes depression. Which refinement is required?

Question 6

A human single-unit study records from the medial temporal lobe in 9 patients with epilepsy and reports neurons that respond selectively to images of the actress Jennifer Aniston. What can be concluded about the representation of familiar people in the human brain?

Question 7

A single patient with a rare lesion is scanned while viewing faces. The contrast of faces versus houses produces a strong left fusiform cluster. The authors publish it as a case study locating the face area. Colleagues note that repeating the scan in the same patient on a second day produces a cluster in a different location. What does this imply?

Question 8

One hertz repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation over the left parietal cortex for 20 minutes reduces performance on a number comparison task for 15 minutes afterwards, while stimulation over the right parietal cortex and sham stimulation have no effect. A reviewer asks how many pulses were delivered and what the after-effect demonstrates.

Question 9

Intraoperative recordings from the subthalamic nucleus in Parkinson patients show that local field potential power in the beta band (13 to 30 Hz) falls when dopaminergic medication is given and when therapeutic stimulation is switched on, and that beta power correlates with rigidity scores. A paper concludes that beta oscillations cause parkinsonian rigidity. Which issue is most important?

Question 10

Under propofol sedation, a volunteer is unresponsive and has low perturbational complexity. Under ketamine, a volunteer is also unresponsive but has complexity values within the range seen in wakefulness, and reports vivid dreams afterwards. What does this dissociation imply for the use of complexity as a consciousness measure?

Question 11

You want to test whether theta-gamma coupling in the human hippocampus is necessary for memory encoding. You have access to intracranial electrodes in epilepsy patients and to repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation. Which design best supports a causal claim?

Question 12

A team proposes to test whether locus coeruleus noradrenergic activity sharpens cortical orientation tuning in humans. They plan to use pupil diameter as the index of locus coeruleus activity, a 7-tesla scan to localise the nucleus, and a placebo-controlled beta blocker to manipulate noradrenergic signalling. Which limitation most needs to be addressed in the design?
