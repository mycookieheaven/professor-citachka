← Russian course pathLevel 15 · Cumulative level test

Cumulative level test: Conversation at speed: the fillers, reactions and arrangements of a real exchange, then the phone, the spoken story, humour, the ты and вы switch and a capstone reply

Check understanding, separately from reading

12 questions · 80% to pass · no timer. This longer test revisits both units in the level. These authored questions assess recognition and application of the taught distinctions, not professional qualification. You may review the lessons and retry. Repeat attempts reuse the question bank; a remembered answer is not proof of transfer to a new situation.

Saving a lesson records reading only. Previous lesson completions have not been converted into passing scores. You can continue reading without a pass; the assessment remains unpassed.

Question 1

A learner writes in a report: «Ну, короче, мы всё сделали». What is wrong, and what is the written equivalent?

Question 2

Someone tells you «Вчера сдал экзамен» (fchee-RA zdal eg-ZA-men) and you answer «Да ладно!». She replies «Правда!». What did your «да ладно» mean to her?

Question 3

Which reply declines a proposal while leaving the door open, and which one closes it: «Вряд ли получится» (vryat lee pa-LOO-cheet-sya) or «Нет» (nyet)?

Question 4

Rearrange this closing into the right order: «до связи», «ладно», «созвонимся». What does the order signal?

Question 5

You propose a walk and your friend answers «Я за, но давай не в восемь, а в десять» (ya ZA, no da-VAY ne VOS-yem, a V DYE-syat). What has she done in two moves?

Question 6

On the phone you need the other person to repeat something. Give the two moves, one neutral and one formal, and say why «Что?» (shto) is a poor choice.

Question 7

A colleague tells a long story and you want to show you are following. Which two short responses do the work, and what do they signal?

Question 8

Which of these does not survive translation into English, and why: «шутка», «да шучу я», «ну ты чего»?

Question 9

A student addresses a professor she has known for a year. She uses ты plus his first name. What has gone wrong, and what are the two forms she needs?

Question 10

Give the four moves of an arrangement, in order, with one Russian frame for each.

Question 11

A message from a friend reads «ты где?». A message from a landlord reads «Вы где?». What do the two pronouns signal beyond number?

Question 12

In the capstone exchange, which single reply would you choose if a colleague says «Слушай, у меня билет лишний, пойдём?» (SLOO-shay, oo mee-NYA bee-LYET LEE-shneey, pay-DYOM) and you are free but unsure about the time?
