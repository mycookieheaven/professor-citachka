Question 1

A settlement sheet shows $150,000.00 approved, $46,207.05 of cash deductions and a $7,500.00 escrow. Write the two numbers the merchant needs and the one number that must never be quoted alone.

Question 2

A debit is returned R02, account closed, on a file with 30 of 40 weekly debits outstanding. The servicer's clerk re-presents it the following week. What is wrong, and what is the consequence under a two-returns-in-ninety-days default clause?

Question 3

A payoff is wired two days late, leaving $165.78 outstanding, and the merchant's lawyer demands a termination statement. What are the secured party's duties and what is the merchant's remedy if it does nothing?

Question 4

A merchant is notified that a 45 percent participation exists and is told to split its monthly payment. It asks you whether it can keep paying the servicer. What is the honest answer, and what document resolves it?

Question 5

A renewal rolls a $57,960.00 balance and $60,000.00 of new money into a $117,960.00 base at 1.36 with a 3 percent fee taken from the new money. The broker calls it a 1.36 deal. Write the two sentences that correct him.

Question 6

A merchant with 92.65 percent debits to deposits and a 1.69 day turnover wants a Monday debit. What do you measure, and what do you recommend?

Question 7

A contract accelerates at will, waives a jury, and requires arbitration 1,900 miles away with a $12,500.00 deposit against a $9,400.00 claim. What may you say, and what may you not say?

Question 8

Three statement PDFs share a creation timestamp, the operating account is 41 days old, revenue rose 240.05 percent and the return shows exactly $116,400.00 more than the statements. The merchant offers to explain. What happens next?

Question 9

A complaint alleges a debit eleven days after payoff, another alleges an undisclosed fee, and a third alleges a call after an opt-out. Write the response plan and the process changes, and say what the 1.40 per 100 files rate does and does not tell you.

Question 10

At day ninety the bank shows fifteen debits, two returns and $140.00 of bank fees, and the servicer's ledger shows $66,924.00 collected against thirteen expected debits of $5,148.00. What reconciles, what does not, and what is the exception?

Question 11

You are handed a file where the settlement sheet omits the escrow from the deducted column and the comparison sheet divides total repayment by the approved amount. Which measure is wrong and by how much, and what is the second error?

Question 12

A file funded on 7 November is audited on 6 February after thirteen weekly debits. Name the three records the audit must reconcile and the one event that would make the whole schedule unenforceable.
