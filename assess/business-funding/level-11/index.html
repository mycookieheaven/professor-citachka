Cumulative level test: Reading the record and producing the artifacts: disclosure items, contract clauses, public filings, tax-basis statements and the auditable deal packet

Check understanding, separately from reading

Question 1

A merchant in one state is offered a fixed-payment advance with a reconciliation clause. Write the two reads the packet needs: the disclosure audit and the clause read, naming what each produces for the other.

Question 2

A search shows an all-assets filing from seven years ago with no continuation, and the tax return shows $96,400 of net profit against deposits of $468,000 with a cash method declared. Write the two findings and the one thing the packet must not conclude.

Question 3

A funder declines the requested amount and offers a smaller sum on a shorter tail at a higher factor, and the merchant asks whether he has been declined. Write the three-part answer.

Question 4

A comparison sheet reports net proceeds of $57,300 for a $60,000 advance with a 1.35 factor over 26 weeks, and the contract shows a $2,700 origination fee and an $895 administrative fee. Correct the sheet and state why the error mattered.

Question 5

An evidence calendar shows eleven statement months current, one month stale by 264 days, and one month with no row content. The merchant says the set is complete because every month is accounted for. Correct him and state the consequence.

Question 6

An outreach draft contains fourteen checkable claims, five of which have no supporting document, an unsubscribe link built yesterday, and a list of 480 addresses. Write the corrections, the tests and the honest volume statement.

Question 7

A vetting file has eleven checks with four verified, three failed and four unresolved, including an unconfirmed registration and a contract template containing a confession of judgment provision. Write the output, the clearance list and the boundary on what the file may say.

Question 8

A packet reaches its decision line and names no reversal point. The reviewer's three tests are source, arithmetic and reversal. Write the review and the fix.

Question 9

A merchant insists on signing today because the seller's quote expires, while the file has one missing statement month, a fee in the offer summary that the contract does not carry, and an unconfirmed registration. Write what the advisor presents and refuses.

Question 10

A reviewer objects that the packet's comparison sheet shows the advance at $143.60 per $100 of net proceeds against a term loan at $124.81, and concludes the packet is recommending the advance. Answer the objection.

Question 11

Write the four sentences a packet's scope block must carry about its own standards and limits, and explain the counting rule that makes one of them checkable.

Question 12

Cumulative capstone. A merchant's file contains an offer with a reconciliation clause, a contract whose prepayment clause is silent, a search showing a lapsed all-assets filing, a cash-method return, a calendar missing one month, an outreach record, and a vetting file with two unresolved rows. Write the packet's decision line and its four conditions.
