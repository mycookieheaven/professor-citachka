Unit quiz: Independence, lines and form: right-hand clocks, keyboard voicings, singing, a second section, and one finished performance

Question 1

At 96 beats per minute a fingerstyle player's thumb sounds one bass note per bar while the fingers play eighth notes above it. What are the two intervals, and what does the ratio imply for practice?

Question 2

A keyboardist plays F, A and C, and then plays F, A and C again an octave higher, describing the second voicing as an inversion that has changed the bass. Which description is correct under the inversion material of lesson 57?

Question 3

A learner can sing the melody reliably alone and can play the accompaniment reliably alone, but the combination fails at every chord change. Which plan follows the evidence cited in lesson 58?

Question 4

A songwriter's second section matches the first in tempo, phrase length and chord vocabulary, but listeners report no sense of arriving somewhere new. Which single change best creates the missing contrast while keeping the section related?

Question 5

A learner exports a MIDI file of the finished piece and sends it as the recording, telling the recipient to listen to the performance. What is the problem, and what does the file contain?
