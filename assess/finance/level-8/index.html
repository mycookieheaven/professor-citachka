Question 1

A loan of $315,000 at 6.90 percent over 240 months has a payment of $2,423.32. The borrower is offered 5.95 percent over a fresh 240 months with closing costs of $5,200 and a 1 percent prepayment penalty. Compute the new payment, the monthly saving, the break-even and the interest saved over the term.

Question 2

A 5/1 adjustable-rate mortgage of $425,000 at 6.10 percent over 360 months has a payment of $2,575.48 and a balance of $395,966.62 after five years. The margin is 2.60 percent, the index at the first reset is 4.30 percent, and the caps are 2/2/6. Compute the reset rate and the new payment.

Question 3

A machine costing $90,000 can be leased for 60 payments of $1,830 or bought with a 60-month loan at 7.75 percent, which costs $1,814.13 a month. Compute the implicit rate in the lease, the total payments under each option, and the difference.

Question 4

A UK exporter invoices EUR 600,000 with payment in 180 days. Spot is 1.0855 dollars per euro and the 180-day forward is 1.0800. Compute the cost of the forward against spot in dollars and as an annualised percentage, then state the unhedged receipt if spot is 1.0600 at payment.

Question 5

A director guarantees 100 percent of a $300,000 facility. The lender estimates a 5 percent annual default probability and 40 percent recovery. Compute the expected annual loss and state what the guarantee does to the director's other borrowing if the facility is called at 8 percent interest-only.

Question 6

A four-year supply contract starts at GBP 360,000 a year with a 3.5 percent annual cap and no floor. CPI runs at 4.5 percent each year. Compute the annual charges and the total over four years.

Question 7

A firm buys equipment for $240,000, depreciates it as five-year MACRS property with the half-year convention, and pays tax at 26 percent. Compute the six annual deductions, the present value of the tax shield at 9 percent, and the tax on a sale for $70,000 after four years.

Question 8

A pension promises GBP 30,000 a year for 25 years, indexed at 2 percent, with the first payment in one year. Compute the present value at a 4 percent discount rate, the value if the pension were level, and the rate implied by a transfer value of GBP 480,000.

Question 9

A UK estate is worth GBP 2,400,000, with the home passing to the children. The nil-rate band is GBP 325,000, the residence nil-rate band is GBP 175,000 and tapers by GBP 1 for every GBP 2 above GBP 2,000,000. Compute the tax and the effective rate, then compare it with a calculation that uses the full GBP 500,000 allowance.

Question 10

A firm lists four commitments: a $400,000 loan at 6.50 percent over 120 months with payments of $4,541.92, a 60-month equipment lease at $2,455 a month, an indexed services contract of $480,000 a year for five years with 4 percent annual escalation, and a guarantee with a 10 percent chance of a $200,000 call in year three. Discount everything at 6 percent and rank the commitments by present value.

Question 11

A firm borrows $400,000 at 6.50 percent over 120 months, paying $4,541.92 a month, and separately signs a three-year services contract with an exit fee of $45,000. In month 24 it wants to exit both. The loan carries a 3 percent prepayment penalty in the first two years and the balance is $339,297.79. Compute the total cost of exit and the payments avoided.

Question 12

A firm has a EUR 800,000 receivable due in 90 days at a spot rate of 1.0750 and a personal guarantee of $250,000 with a 4 percent default probability and 30 percent recovery. The euro falls to 1.0300 and the guarantee is called at 8 percent interest-only. Compute the currency loss, the expected loss on the guarantee and the annual cash cost of a call.
