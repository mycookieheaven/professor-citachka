Question 1

A borrower has $196,000 outstanding on a loan at 6.25 percent with 180 monthly payments left, so the payment is $1,680.55. A lender offers 5.50 percent over a fresh 180 months, charging closing costs of $4,200 and a prepayment penalty of 1.5 percent of the balance. Compute the new payment, the monthly saving and the number of months before the upfront cost is recovered.

Question 2

A 5/1 adjustable-rate mortgage of $280,000 started at 5.75 percent over 360 months, giving a payment of $1,634.00. At the first reset the index is 5.20 percent, the margin is 2.50 percent, the initial cap is 2.00 percentage points and the balance is $259,734.12 with 300 months left. Compute the rate that applies and the new payment.

Question 3

A business can lease a machine for 48 monthly payments of $1,540 with no residual, or borrow $64,000 at 8.00 percent over 48 months and buy it. Total lease payments are $73,920 and total loan payments are $74,996.50. Compute the interest rate implicit in the lease and state which option is cheaper.

Question 4

A US exporter expects EUR 900,000 in 120 days. The spot rate is 1.0790 dollars per euro and the 120-day forward is 1.0725. Compute the dollars locked in by the forward, the cost of cover against the spot rate, and the annualised cost. Then state the outcome if the spot rate is 1.0400 at payment.

Question 5

A owner personally guarantees 100 percent of a $400,000 business loan. The lender's own data imply a 3 percent annual probability of default and 25 percent recovery in the event of default. Compute the expected loss on the guarantee and the annual debt service the owner would face if the guarantee were called at 8 percent interest-only.
