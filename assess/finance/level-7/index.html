Question 1

A borrower is offered a $12,000 two-year instalment loan at an assumed 6 percent, a 60-month version of the same loan at the same rate, and a $12,000 advance priced at a factor rate of 1.25 repaid over 24 equal monthly payments. The offer sheet states that all three cost about the same because the first two share a rate and the third is quoted as a multiple. Using the level's methods, give the three costs and state which single assumption the comparison depends on.

Question 2

A facility of $40,000 offered for 36 months at an assumed 8 percent carries $1,300 of fees, while a competing offer of the same amount for the same term carries a 6.75 percent rate and $150 of fees. A summary ranks the second offer below the first on cost because 6.75 percent is lower than 8 percent plus fees. Check the ranking using total cost against money received, and say whether the summary's reason holds.

Question 3

A firm reports a month with $66,000 invoiced, $58,900 collected, $47,500 of recognised expenses including $1,200 of depreciation, payables up $2,300 and $4,100 of inventory bought for cash. It holds $27,000 of cash and its average monthly net cash outflow is $1,500. Separately, a quarterly insurance deposit of $4,000 falls in one month of each quarter. Compute the operating cash, the runway and the effect of the deposit month.

Question 4

A firm with $520,000 of assets, $315,000 of liabilities including $180,000 of floating-rate debt, $92,000 of earnings before interest and taxes and $23,000 of interest is negotiating a loan whose covenant requires debt-to-equity to stay below 1.80 and times interest earned to stay above 3.50. A vice-president says the firm has room on both. Test that claim under a two point rate rise and a $60,000 expansion funded by new debt.

Question 5

A product sells at $52 with a $31 variable cost and $26,000 of monthly fixed costs. The owner wants both to cut the price to $46 and to add a $1,850 monthly fixed cost for a delivery vehicle. Compute the break-even volume under each change and under both together, and say what the combination requires.

Question 6

A household plans $3,180 of essentials, $1,240 of variable spending and $420 of sinking funds, which includes $150 a month for an $1,800 annual premium, against $5,400 of income. The month delivered $3,290 of essentials, $1,690 of variable spending, the planned sinking funds, and an $980 repair, and its worst week showed a $1,250 shortfall because income arrived on the first and $2,450 of bills fell on the twenty-eighth. Diagnose the plan and state the two changes it implies.

Question 7

A business collects 9.5 percent tax on $62,000 of monthly taxable sales, and pays four employees $2,850 each with illustrative federal withholding of $250, employee social security at 6.2 percent and Medicare at 1.45 percent. It has been using both collected amounts to fund seasonal inventory. Compute the amounts at stake over a year and state the exposure.

Question 8

A business holds a 2026 income tax return with supporting schedules, employment tax records for 2026, the invoice and proof of payment for a machine bought in 2026 and still in use, a file of customer credit applications, and a spreadsheet listing expenses with no underlying receipts. State what may be destroyed and when, and what must be created or disposed of instead.

Question 9

A memo compares option X, costing $50,000 and returning $11,000 a year for seven years, with option Y, costing $75,000 and returning $15,000 a year for seven years, and reports only that Y is preferable because it costs more and returns more each year. Rewrite the conclusion using an assumed 9 percent discount rate, and state what the memo must also contain.

Question 10

A saver is told that an assumed 7 percent nominal return over a long horizon will preserve purchasing power comfortably. Inflation is assumed at 3 percent, the return is taxable at an assumed 22 percent marginal rate, and a competitor's product is quoted at 1.75 percent per month on a declining balance. Compute the real after-tax return and identify which quoted figure belongs to which measurement.

Question 11

A firm has revenue of $720,000, a 32 percent gross margin, $168,000 of fixed costs and $150,000 of floating-rate debt at an assumed rate. Compute the operating profit, break-even revenue and cushion, then test a 10 percent fall in revenue and a four percentage point fall in margin, and state which assumption the business depends on more.

Question 12

A lender's summary of a financing offer states that the rate is a 1.30 factor which annualises to 26 percent, that the borrower receives $48,750 on a $50,000 facility because of $1,250 of fees, and that the deal is cheaper than a 12 percent instalment loan of the same size and term because the total repayment is lower. Identify every defect in the summary and give the correct treatment of each figure.
