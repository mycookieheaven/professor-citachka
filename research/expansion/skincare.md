# Skincare expansion handoff

## Delivered scope
One new level, two units, ten substantive lessons (21–30), five fresh questions per unit and twelve fresh cumulative application questions. This bounded two-unit release does not fulfill the long-term 100-unit target.

Files: `src/lib/course-packs/skincare.json` and `research/expansion/skincare.md`.

## Prerequisites and safeguards
Read `practical.ts` and the published slicing in `programs.ts`; current skincare IDs end at 20. New work extends ingredient literacy, barrier care, acne/pigment distinctions, tolerance limitations, sanitation, image literacy and routine auditing rather than repeating introductions. All cases are fictional; no learner medical details. Education only: no diagnosis, individualized prescribing, drug substitutions, home peels, allergy challenges, or promises of safety. Emergency and referral boundaries are explicit. Pregnancy guidance emphasizes accurate exposure history and clinician coordination without equating prior exposure with harm.

## Source grounding and actual verification
- FDA regulatory definitions distinguish cosmetics, drugs, registration and safety obligations.[9]
- AAD dry-skin care supports gentle methods and referral for persistent problems.[10]
- AAD home product observation is distinguished from professional patch testing and is not a safety guarantee.[11]
- AAD sunscreen guidance supports label interpretation, realistic reapplication and combined protection.[12]
- FDA explains storage, contamination and shelf-life limitations.[13]
- AAD pregnancy guidance supports clinician reconciliation and retinoid/hydroquinone caution; this pack does not reproduce a personalized regimen or broad unsupported ingredient-family bans.[14]
- AAD rash guidance supports emergency symptoms and non-color-only triage.[15]
- AAD acne guidance supports separating active disease, residual pigment and procedure risks in darker skin tones.[16]
- AAD self-exam guidance supports referral for differing, changing, itching or bleeding spots without diagnosing them.[17]
- FDA consumer reporting guidance supports factual reports of reactions or product-quality problems without claiming proven causation; consumer reporting is distinguished from industry obligations.[18]

## Arithmetic and boundaries
No derived clinical calculation is presented. SPF values, 40/80-minute water-resistance designations, two-hour outdoor reapplication guidance, and seven-to-ten-day home-test guidance are source-provided values, not computed personal exposure limits. No safe sunbathing duration or drug dose is calculated.

## Remaining verification
Parent owns application integration, renderer tests and Vercel production verification. No shared source, tests, deployment, browser, localhost, or GitHub work was performed. Product use, clinical outcomes, learner competence and live route behavior have not been tested. The binary assessments support reasoning practice only and do not qualify anyone to diagnose or prescribe.
