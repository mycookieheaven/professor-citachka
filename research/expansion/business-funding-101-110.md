## Funding-honesty compliance

- **A factor is never described as a rate or an APR**, and no factor is converted into an annual figure anywhere in the pack. Lesson 106 explicitly refuses a rate column and a winner column; lesson 101 refuses to treat a statutory annualized-rate disclosure item as an APR.
- **Net proceeds versus gross** is separated in 101, 102, 105, 106 and 110, with the denominator of every percentage named.
- **Timing assumptions** are stated in 102 (true-up tails), 105, 106 (reconciliation footnote), 107 (validity windows) and 104 (cash-versus-accrual timing and § 481(a) items).
- **Education versus individualized advice**: lesson 101, 102, 105, 108 and 109 each state that the legal and compliance questions (which regime applies, whether notification duties attach for a particular applicant, channel coverage, clause enforceability) go to counsel, and every lesson repeats that underwriting judgement belongs to the funder and not the advisor.
- **No guaranteed approvals, false affiliations, manufactured urgency, hidden costs or pressure tactics**: lesson 108 deletes exactly those claims from an outreach draft and is the pack's worked example of doing so; lesson 95's earlier prohibitions are assumed and extended rather than restated as boilerplate.
- **MCA classification varies by jurisdiction** is handled by structure rather than by assertion: lesson 101 works item by item from two state statutes and one registration regime, names each regime's scope, and never states a nationwide rule. No claim is made that any state's statute applies to a particular file.
- **Borrowed supervisory reasoning is labelled**: lesson 110 states that the OCC handbook and the FTC policy statement bind banks and govern consumer-facing advertising respectively, and that neither binds a non-bank advisor.
