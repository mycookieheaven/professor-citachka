## Funding-honesty compliance

- **A factor is never described as an interest rate or an APR.** Commercial costs are stated as dollars per $100 of net proceeds, total repayment, payment and frequency, or a date. The one nominal rate (lesson 93) attaches to an illustrative amortising term loan with a monthly payment, a total of payments and total interest, explicitly labelled illustrative — and the lesson then says the two metrics cannot be lined up. Lessons 92, 95, 96 and 99 require the advisor's own note to say the same.
- **Gross advance versus net proceeds** is separated in 92, 93, 96, 97 and 100, where the $22,690.34 figure is explicitly net proceeds.
- **Fees, remittance, reconciliation, guarantees, UCC filings, stacking and jurisdiction** are all addressed: SBA upfront-versus-annual fee context and the prohibition on charging lender-service-provider compensation to the applicant (96); remittances expressed in weeks and dollars throughout; reconciliation and true-up defined via the Virginia definitions and the FTC's stacking and true-up concerns referenced in earlier packs; guarantees in the SI 103.4 refusal discussion and the write-up/aggregate-obligation work in 97; UCC search as a screen field and a condition (91, 92, 99); stacking and aggregate load (97); Virginia and SBA programme terms each stated inside their own scope with no claim of nationwide application.
- **Merchant pressure is handled honestly and without tactics.** Lesson 96 defines a servable list and a refusal list, refuses the use of manufactured urgency as a licence, requires the commission to be stated in the conversation, and teaches a three-move close rather than persistence. Nothing in the pack instructs or implies deception, coercion, concealment, hidden costs or pressure: the only appearances of phrases such as "conceal fees", "guarantee of approval" and "APR" are inside lists of things the learner must **not** do, or in statements that a factor is not an APR. A scanning pass found no other pressure or urgency phrasing.
- **Commission conflicts are disclosed honestly** in five separate places: the framework's written-down asymmetry (91), the memo's own compensation line (92), the price-versus-structure comparison where the advisor must state what they earn on each route (93, 98), the pressure lesson's explicit $3,000 commission against the merchant's $748 shortfall (96), the decline letter's compensation line (99) and the capstone's sixth part (100).
- **No approval, funding, rate, relief or outcome is promised anywhere.** Declining and not-yet are presented as legitimate, useful outputs with reasons and conditions; lesson 99 requires that no prediction be made about any other decision-maker.

## Unresolved limitations (for the parent)

1. **The staged pack is outside every runner's glob by construction.** The real `attachCoursePacks` function was exercised against this pack (see above, result `valid`) and the invariants were replicated in Python, but `scripts/validate-packs.ts` and `scripts/verify-source-urls.py` both read only the top-level `src/lib/course-packs/*.json`, so neither can see `staged/`. The parent should run the repository's validator and the vitest suite after moving the file and registering it.
2. **The pack is in `staged/` and is therefore not loaded.** `course-pack-registry.ts` imports explicit paths and never glob-loads, so nothing is published until the parent moves the file into `src/lib/course-packs/` and imports it after `businessFunding8190`. The registry comment requires teaching order, which this pack satisfies.
3. **The 81–90 handoff's summary of 13 CFR 120.150 is now partly out of date.** The current text (88 FR 21085, 10 April 2023) reads "appropriate and prudent generally acceptable commercial credit analysis processes and procedures consistent with those used for their similarly-sized, non-SBA guaranteed commercial loans". The earlier handoff paraphrased it as "prudent generally acceptable commercial credit analysis". Both are recorded here so the parent can reconcile any wording in lessons 81–90 if it decides to.
4. **Two hosts behave inconsistently to scripted access.** `ecfr.gov` served a CAPTCHA interstitial to one fetch with a minimal user agent while a desktop user agent returned the page; an automated link checker using a bare user agent may report the interstitial as content. `consumerfinance.gov` returned 200 to a plain scripted fetch this session, contrary to the caution in this batch's brief, so its reading notes say that no 403 was observed rather than repeating a caveat.
5. **`occ.treas.gov` worked this session.** The 81–90 handoff recorded the OCC host as unusable after repeated not-found responses; the Rating Credit Risk PDF resolved cleanly (HTTP 200) here, and `occ.gov` was used for the Concentrations booklet and the 2006 CRE bulletin. A reviewer should confirm the OCC host it prefers before merging, since the same booklet exists under both `occ.gov` and `occ.treas.gov` paths.
6. **Legal and compliance questions are flagged, not resolved.** Whether a purchase of receivables is a loan in a given state, guarantee enforceability, the reach of Regulation B's business-credit tracks, the applicability of a state disclosure regime to a particular broker, and the treatment of an undisclosed obligation all require qualified review. The lessons say so and refer the merchant to a lawyer; a reviewer may want the caveats strengthened, particularly for lessons 95 and 99.
7. **Supervisory material is borrowed reasoning and is dated.** The OCC booklets are 2001 (updated 2017 and 2025 for specific changes), the Concentrations booklet is October 2020, the CRE bulletin is December 2006, SR 11-7 is April 2011 and section 3.2 of the FDIC manual is the 3-2026 edition. None of it binds a non-bank advisor, and every reading note says so. The FTC deception statement is a 1983 policy statement.
8. **No real client data and no practitioner review.** All merchants, amounts, terms, dates and dialogues are fictional. No practising underwriter, broker or lawyer reviewed the content, and passing these assessments is explicitly not professional certification.
9. **Not delivered in this batch:** registration, renderer or application tests, audit-script updates, production build, deployment, HTTP readback, and any visual or audio verification — all parent-owned. No claim is made that this pack is live.
