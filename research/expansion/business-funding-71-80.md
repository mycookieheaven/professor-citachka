## Funding-accuracy compliance

- A factor is never described as an interest rate or an annual percentage rate. Every commercial cost is stated as a dollar amount per hundred dollars of net proceeds, a total repayment amount, a payment amount and frequency, or a date. Lessons 75, 77 and 79 explicitly compare structures this way, and no annualised figure is computed anywhere in the pack.
- Gross advance and net proceeds are separated wherever they appear (71, 75, 77, 79, 80), with fees named as line items; lesson 77 shows a payoff inside a renewal and lesson 79 shows the same advance with a $1,600 fee deduction implied by the net-proceeds arithmetic.
- Fees, remittance cadence, reconciliation, guarantees, UCC filings, stacking and jurisdiction variation are all addressed directly: SBA upfront versus annual service fees that the lender may not charge to the borrower; SBA prepayment penalties on long maturities; remittance stated in weeks and dollars; the reconciliation defined operationally in lessons 73 and 79; personal guarantees with joint-and-several exposure and the RAM Capital allegations as the boundary; UCC perfection, purchase-money priority with the 20-day window, five-year effectiveness and the six-month continuation window; stacking in lessons 73, 77 and 80; Virginia, California and the jurisdictional caveats stated as the sources state them, with no claim of nationwide application.
- No approval, funding, rate, relief or outcome is promised. Every commercial figure is labelled fictional or illustrative. Declining is presented as a legitimate and often useful conclusion, with reasons and conditions. Nothing suggests deception, coercion, concealment or manufactured urgency; lesson 78 instructs a refusal to assist with concealment, and lesson 79 removes unsupported speed claims and manufactured deadlines.

5. **Legal and compliance questions are flagged, not resolved.** Loan-versus-purchase characterisation, guarantee enforceability, filing priority in a specific state, and whether the FDCPA or a state collection regime reaches a particular obligation all require qualified review. The lessons say so and refer the merchant to a lawyer; a reviewer may want the caveats strengthened further.
6. **Marketplace statistics are second-hand and dated where they are.** The 59% personal-guarantee figure is from the Federal Reserve's 2026 Report on Employer Firms (a 2025 convenience-sample survey); the FTC material is a February 2020 staff perspective. Neither is presented as a current market rate or as a forecast.
7. **No real client data, no practitioner input.** All merchants, amounts, terms, dates and dialogues are fictional; no practising underwriter, broker or lawyer reviewed the content, and passing these assessments is explicitly not professional certification.
8. **Not delivered in this batch:** registration, renderer or application tests, audit-script updates, production build, deployment, HTTP readback, and any visual or audio verification (all parent-owned). No claim is made that this pack is live.
