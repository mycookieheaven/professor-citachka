An esthetics-minded, evidence-aware course in skin care, ingredients, routines, sanitation, and professional judgment, from the first gentle cleanser to independent routine analysis.

Beautiful skin care is not a race to use the strongest product. It is trained observation: know what the skin needs, protect what is working, and intervene with precision.

Professional boundary

Esthetics knowledge, medical discernment.

This department teaches cosmetic care, hygiene, ingredient literacy, and routine design. It does not diagnose skin disease or replace dermatology. Painful, scarring, spreading, infected-looking, or rapidly changing concerns deserve medical evaluation.
