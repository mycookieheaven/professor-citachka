A beginner-first path from the first steady beat to playing guitar and MIDI keyboard, then using FL Studio to turn those skills into music.

We do not begin by trying to be impressive. We begin by learning to hear, count, touch, and repeat, then the music has somewhere solid to stand.

Your first rule

Small practice beats heroic confusion.

On a busy day, do five minutes: count four beats, make one clean guitar chord or keyboard melody, and stop while you still know what you are doing. Consistent, accurate repetitions teach hands and ears far more reliably than an occasional exhausted marathon.

Beyond the foundation

Your expanding curriculum

This department does not stop at beginner level. Each stage adds complexity, independence, and real-world application.

  1. Level 1Foundation: rhythm, theory, first guitar and keyboard technique
